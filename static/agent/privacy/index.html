LeBron privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

LeBron is a private assistant that Jason Miazga runs for his own family (about). This policy covers the Google user data it uses.

What it accesses

Read-only access to Google Calendar (calendar.readonly), through the assistant's own Google account. That account sees only the calendars that family members have chosen to share with it, plus public calendars such as holidays and team schedules. It never creates, changes or deletes events.

How it's used

Only to write the family's daily emails: each day's events, leave-by times, the week ahead and scheduling conflicts. The emails go only to family members.

To write them, the assistant sends that day's event details to Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms and doesn't use them to train its models.

Storage

The sign-in token and the assistant's working files stay on Jason's own computer, outside any public code. The daily emails are deleted from the assistant's mailbox after 14 days.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone beyond the steps above.

LeBron's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Stopping access

Anyone can stop sharing their calendar with the assistant in Google Calendar's settings at any time. The assistant's own access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
